INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT — SCHEDULE B
Meridian Public Employees' Retirement System
Mandate: Core Fixed Income (separate account)
Benchmark: Bloomberg US Aggregate Bond Index

B.1 Concentration. No more than five percent (5%) of the market value of the
Account shall be invested in the securities of any single issuer, provided that
this limitation shall not apply to obligations issued or guaranteed by the United
States government or its agencies, or to cash and cash equivalents.

B.2 Credit Quality. Securities purchased for the Account shall carry a minimum
rating of Baa3 by Moody's or BBB- by Standard & Poor's at the time of purchase.
Where the two agencies assign split ratings, the lower shall govern. Securities
downgraded below the minimum after purchase need not be sold immediately, but
shall be disposed of within ninety (90) days unless the Client agrees otherwise in
writing.

B.3 Duration. The effective duration of the Account shall be maintained within
plus or minus one (1) year of the effective duration of the Benchmark.

B.4 Prohibited Instruments. The Manager shall not invest the Account in private
placements, commodities, or direct real estate. The Manager is not prohibited from
using interest rate futures and interest rate swaps solely for the purpose of
managing portfolio duration, and shall not employ such instruments to create
economic leverage.

B.5 Restricted Securities. The Manager shall not purchase any security appearing
on the Client's restricted securities list, as delivered to the Manager and
updated from time to time in accordance with Appendix IV.

B.6 Management Fee. The Manager shall be paid a fee, billed quarterly in arrears,
of 22 basis points per annum on the first $100 million of average daily market
value and 18 basis points per annum on amounts above that level.

B.7 Reporting. The Manager shall deliver a monthly performance and holdings
report within ten (10) business days of month end, and a quarterly attribution
report including duration and sector positioning against the Benchmark within
twenty (20) business days of quarter end.

B.8 Custody. Assets of the Account shall be held by Northern Trust Company as
custodian. The Manager shall transmit trade instructions to the custodian by
11:00 a.m. Eastern on trade date plus one.

B.9 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in
accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict
of laws principles.

INVESTMENT GUIDELINES — EXHIBIT A
The Calder Foundation
Mandate: Global Equity, screened (separate account)
Benchmark: MSCI ACWI

A.1 Sector Exposure. Exposure to any single GICS sector shall not exceed the
weight of that sector in the Benchmark by more than seven hundred fifty (750)
basis points.

A.2 Emerging Markets. Not more than fifteen percent (15%) of the market value of
the Account may be invested in issuers domiciled in countries classified as
emerging markets by the Benchmark provider.

A.3 Exclusionary Screens. The Account shall exclude issuers deriving more than
five percent (5%) of revenue from thermal coal extraction, and issuers deriving
any revenue from the manufacture of controversial weapons. Screening shall be
applied at the issuer level.

A.4 Tracking Error. The Manager shall manage the Account with a view to
maintaining tracking error against the Benchmark of no more than four hundred
(400) basis points.

A.5 Cash. The Account shall hold not less than one percent (1%) and not more than
five percent (5%) of market value in cash and cash equivalents, tested at month
end.

A.6 Currency. The Manager shall not hedge currency exposure except as otherwise
agreed in writing between the parties.

A.7 Management Fee. Forty (40) basis points per annum on average daily market
value, billed quarterly in arrears, subject to an annual minimum fee of $150,000.

A.8 Reporting. The Manager shall provide a quarterly report including holdings,
performance against the Benchmark, and a summary of screening exceptions
identified during the period.
